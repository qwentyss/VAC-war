# Test Plan — Post-Update Test 2

## 1. Test Information

- **Test ID:** Post-Update Test 2
- **Research phase:** Post-update
- **Related hypothesis:** [Theory 2](../../../research/post-update/second-theory/theory-2.md)
- **Planned start date:** October 18, 2026
- **Planned end date:** October 25, 2026
- **Status:** Planned

## 2. Objective

Assess the conceptual claim that displayed opponent information can be compared with information a player could ordinarily perceive during gameplay.

The research focuses on documenting the relationship between visual conditions, audible cues, and the information available in the recorded evidence.

It does not presume a particular VAC implementation or detection mechanism.

## 3. Research Questions

1. Can the visibility of opponents be assessed consistently from the available recordings?
2. Are smoke and flash effects documented clearly enough to evaluate their impact on ordinary player perception?
3. Can audible cues be distinguished from situations in which no relevant sound is established in the recording?
4. Which observations support, contradict, or leave the hypothesis unresolved?
5. What limitations prevent reliable conclusions?

## 4. Scope

The research is limited to documenting the hypothesis and evaluating evidence collected in an authorized environment.

It does not attempt to determine how to conceal cheating software, circumvent anti-cheat detection, or avoid enforcement.

## 5. Evidence Collection

Where permitted, record:

- Date and time of each observation.
- Game build and relevant software versions.
- Map and general gameplay conditions.
- Whether the session was completed or interrupted.
- Available video or other authorized evidence.
- Relevant visual conditions, including smoke and flash effects.
- Audible cues that can be established from the recording.
- Missing evidence, uncertainty, and alternative explanations.

Avoid recording account credentials or other sensitive information.

## 6. Evaluation Criteria

Classify each finding as one of the following:

- **Supports:** The recorded evidence is consistent with the stated hypothesis.
- **Contradicts:** The recorded evidence conflicts with the stated hypothesis.
- **Inconclusive:** The evidence is insufficient or ambiguous.
- **Not evaluated:** The required observation or evidence is unavailable.

Do not infer the cause of a ban from timing alone.

## 7. Completion Criteria

The research may be marked complete when:

- The available observations have been documented.
- Evidence has been reviewed for completeness.
- Limitations and missing data have been recorded.
- Conclusions are proportionate to the evidence.
- The final assessment is added to `results.md`.

## 8. Expected Output

A structured record of observations and a conclusion stating whether the available evidence supports, contradicts, or leaves Theory 2 unresolved.

No particular result is assumed in advance.
