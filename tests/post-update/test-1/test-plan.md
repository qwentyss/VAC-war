# Test Plan — Post-Update Test 1

## 1. Objective

Evaluate the hypothesis described in [Post-Update Theory 1](../../../research/post-update/first-theory/theory-1.md) using documented observations.

The experiment aims to determine whether the available evidence supports, contradicts, or leaves the hypothesis unresolved.

## 2. Hypothesis

**Primary hypothesis:** The reported behavior following the October 9, 2026 update may be related to process-name or process-metadata inspection.

This hypothesis remains unverified. The test must not assume that VAC uses keyword-based detection or that a specific process name causes a ban.

## 3. Planned Sample

**Target sample:** 15 matches

**Planned window:** October 16–25, 2026

Record the actual number of completed matches. If the planned sample cannot be completed, document the reason.

## 4. Scope

The research will document:

- Relevant game and environment versions.
- The conditions under which observations are made.
- Observable behavior and available logs.
- Differences between baseline and test observations.
- Alternative explanations and limitations.

The experiment cannot directly establish VAC's internal implementation without independent evidence.

## 5. Procedure

1. Record the environment details before testing.
2. Verify that the environment and observation process are documented.
3. Record baseline observations where available.
4. Conduct only authorized testing in an appropriate environment.
5. Record each match separately.
6. Record timestamps and observable events.
7. Document missing data and inconsistencies.
8. Review the observations after the planned sample is complete.
9. Write a conclusion that reflects the strength and limits of the evidence.

## 6. Observation Fields

For each match, record:

- Match number.
- Date and time, including time zone.
- Relevant environment versions.
- Whether the planned conditions were maintained.
- Observable detection or enforcement indicators.
- Evidence references, where available.
- Notes and potential confounding factors.

Do not include account credentials, authentication tokens, or other sensitive information.

## 7. Evaluation Criteria

### Supports the Hypothesis

Use this classification only when observations provide reproducible evidence specifically consistent with a prediction of the hypothesis and alternative explanations have been considered.

### Contradicts the Hypothesis

Use this classification when reliable observations conflict with a specific prediction of the hypothesis.

### Inconclusive

Use this classification when evidence is insufficient, inconsistent, or compatible with multiple explanations.

### No Ban Observed

If no ban is observed across 15 matches, report that exact result. Do not automatically classify it as confirmation of the hypothesis.

A lack of visible enforcement does not establish that detection did not occur.

## 8. Reporting

Record the observations in [Results and Observations](results.md).

The final assessment must distinguish direct observations from interpretations and clearly state what cannot be concluded.
