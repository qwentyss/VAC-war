# VAC Research

Research and documentation of Valve Anti-Cheat (VAC), focusing on detection mechanisms, behavioral changes, and potential bypass concepts.

Created on October 11, 2026, following the October 9 VAC update, which reportedly rendered a significant portion of existing cheats unusable.

## Stage 1: Theories of VAC Before and After the Update

The first stage focuses on analyzing VAC before and after the October 9, 2026 update.

### Topics Covered

- Theories about VAC's detection mechanisms.
- Possible changes introduced by the update.
- Why existing cheats may have stopped working.
- Technical observations, diagrams, and screenshots.
- Research hypotheses and supporting evidence.

> **Note:** All theories remain unconfirmed unless supported by reliable evidence.
