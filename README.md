# VAC Research

**Research and technical documentation of Valve Anti-Cheat (VAC) in Counter-Strike 2.**

Created on October 11, 2026, following reports of significant changes to cheat compatibility after the October 9, 2026 update.

This project investigates VAC's detection mechanisms, examines potential behavioral changes, and documents technical theories based on publicly available research and observations.

---

## Research Areas

- VAC architecture and detection mechanisms.
- Potential differences before and after updates.
- Process and module analysis.
- Technical observations and visual explanations.
- Research hypotheses, supporting evidence, and limitations.

## Research Methodology

The project distinguishes between three types of information:

- **Verified:** Supported by reliable sources or reproducible observations.
- **Unconfirmed:** A plausible hypothesis that requires further investigation.
- **Speculative:** An interpretation without sufficient supporting evidence.

Technical claims should include relevant references whenever possible. Observations from a specific game version should not automatically be assumed to apply to other versions.

The theories presented here are not official descriptions of VAC's internal implementation. No specific detection mechanism or automatic ban condition should be considered confirmed without sufficient evidence.
