## Objective

Document and assess the conceptual hypothesis described in Post-Update Theory 2.

The hypothesis concerns whether information displayed by a visual overlay corresponds to information available through ordinary in-game perception, including visibility, smoke, flash effects, and audible cues.

This is exploratory research. It does not assume that the proposed behavior is technically feasible or that it affects anti-cheat enforcement.

## Important Limitations

- The hypothesis has not been independently verified.
- Visual behavior alone does not establish whether software is detected by VAC.
- A ban or other enforcement event does not identify its cause.
- Observations from a limited sample cannot establish universal behavior.
- Incomplete recordings and uncertain visibility or audio conditions may affect interpretation.
